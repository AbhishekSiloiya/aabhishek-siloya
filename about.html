My background

Strategy, with responsibility for delivery.

For more than two decades, I have worked in product, digital strategy and transformation. Earlier consulting work with organisations including Heathrow, Japan Airlines and Sonepar took me across industries, markets and ways of doing business.

That experience taught me to look beyond the plan: who will carry it out, what customers need, and whether the business can support the change.

As the founder of Bhuzen and VaxGuard, I also know how personal those choices become. Time, money and trust are yours to commit. I bring that perspective to my coaching.
