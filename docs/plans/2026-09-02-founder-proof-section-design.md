# Founder-Facing Proof Section

## Decision

Reframe “Selected prior work” from a client-and-metric register into evidence that Aabhishek's present counsel is grounded in delivery. The founder should understand the relevance before encountering the numbers.

## Content hierarchy

1. Eyebrow: “Selected prior work”.
2. Thesis: “Before counsel, there was delivery.”
3. One short bridge connecting more than two decades of product and digital strategy to capital, capability and execution.
4. Three proofs, labelled by what they demonstrate: commercial opportunity, operating scale and execution velocity.
5. A restrained cross-market line naming the United Kingdom, Europe and Japan and selected organisations from earlier consulting work.
6. A provenance note stating that these are not clients of the current private coaching practice.

## Evidence boundary

Retain only the existing supplied claims: £50m+ at Heathrow with investment secured; $1bn platform transaction value at Sonepar within eight months across four European markets; Japan Airlines delivery brought forward from an estimated six-to-eight months to around four; and the supplied organisation and geography names. Do not imply that Aabhishek independently caused each outcome or that the organisations are current coaching clients.

## Visual direction

Preserve the warm-stone section and cardless layout. Give the thesis the dominant left column. On the right, use three disciplined evidence rows with a small relevance label, a large outcome and concise factual explanation. Place the cross-market credential and provenance note beneath the rows, separated by one rule. No logos, photography, badges or additional motion.

## Acceptance criteria

- A founder can understand the section's argument by reading only the thesis, relevance labels and figures.
- Company names support the proof rather than dominating it.
- The provenance distinction is visible without reading like a legal disclaimer.
- No new factual claim is introduced.
- Mobile retains a clear, single-column reading order with no horizontal overflow.
