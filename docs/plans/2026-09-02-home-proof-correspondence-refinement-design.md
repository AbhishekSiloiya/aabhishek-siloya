# Home Proof and Correspondence Refinement

## Decision

Two focused changes to the local Home page:

1. Replace the Japan Airlines display metric with **3 months**. Preserve accuracy in the explanation: “International personalisation delivered in approximately three months, against an estimated six-to-eight-month timeline.”
2. Replace the Private Correspondence headline with **Begin privately.** The kicker and the two paths already explain the section, so the headline should orient rather than repeat them.

## Visual thesis

Quiet assurance: fewer words, a lower headline volume and a tighter transition from invitation to action.

## Content plan

- Proof: metric first, organisation second, factual qualification third.
- Correspondence: short invitation, two distinct paths, aligned actions and a compact discretion note.

## Interaction thesis

Retain the existing restrained reveal and link affordances. Add no new motion; this refinement is about rhythm and hierarchy.

## Layout

On desktop, reduce the correspondence headline scale and its lower margin, shorten the minimum height of both columns, and tighten internal heading/body gaps. Keep both actions bottom-aligned. On mobile, preserve a single-column flow with compact spacing and comfortable tap targets.

## Evidence boundary

The user supplied the revised delivery duration. The visible metric is concise, while “approximately” preserves the qualification. No other figures or client relationships change.
