# Work authority page — design direction

## Objective

Turn the Work page from a résumé-shaped list into evidence that a founder, owner or senior executive can trust quickly. The page must establish commercial judgement, operating depth and discretion without implying that prior organisations are clients of the current coaching practice.

## Audience and desired response

- Founder, owner, family-enterprise principal or C-suite leader.
- They should understand within one screen that Aabhishek has carried consequential decisions at scale.
- They should leave with a reason to start a private conversation, not with a complete career history.

## Editorial thesis

**A record of decisions made real.**

The page leads with consequence rather than a self-description. Four short operating chapters carry the proof. The organisations register supplies breadth; it does not become the story.

## Page sequence

1. **Opening** — a compact statement of the record and its relevance.
2. **Prior organisations** — all approved names, shown as one restrained monochrome register.
3. **Selected decisions** — four chapters: Heathrow, Sonepar, Japan Airlines and Maybourne Hotel Group.
4. **Founder/operator bridge** — direct acknowledgement that Aabhishek has also carried the uncertainty and responsibility of building his own ventures.
5. **TRVLR advisory note** — one qualified current-advisory example with a real artefact and no inflated outcome claim.
6. **Private close** — a concise invitation for decisions that carry material weight.

## Visual system

- Genre: editorial, quiet-luxury.
- Macrostructure: Long Document, adapted as a private casebook.
- Theme: retain the site’s warm paper, near-black ink and restrained bronze accent.
- Typography: Newsreader for consequence; Manrope for evidence, context and navigation.
- Rhythm: compact opening, deliberate hairline rules, 60–70 character reading measure and no oversized section slogans.
- Imagery: one TRVLR artefact only. No portrait repetition on the Work page.
- Motion: one subtle entrance rhythm for evidence rows; no parallax, counters or decorative animation.

## Content rules

- Use only source-backed outcomes.
- Use “under four months” for Japan Airlines on this page because the underlying record supports approximately 3.5–4 months; do not convert it to a 50% claim here.
- Keep the qualifier adjacent to the organisation register: prior operating work, not current independent-practice clients.
- Name every approved organisation: Maybourne Hotel Group, Heathrow, Japan Airlines, Sonepar, ASDA, European Tours, Howdens, Carnival Cruise, ASOS and BLU Digital.
- Treat unverified or ambiguous logo artwork as a temporary typographic nameplate rather than fabricating an official mark.

## Success criteria

- The first viewport answers: what kind of record, why it matters, and whose record it is.
- The strongest evidence is readable in under 45 seconds.
- Every metric has context and consequence.
- The complete organisation register is present without becoming a loud SaaS-style logo wall.
- Desktop and mobile retain the same reading order, with no clipped type or horizontal overflow.

## Approved refinement — editorial compression

The first implementation proved the narrative structure but retained too much ceremonial scale. The refinement keeps the six-part sequence and all approved evidence while making the page feel sharper and more assured.

- Reduce total page height by approximately 20–25% at laptop and mobile widths.
- Cap the opening title below 80px on desktop and below 58px on mobile.
- Keep section titles materially quieter than the opening title.
- Reduce section intervals, grid gaps, evidence-row padding and CTA height without compressing body copy below a readable 13px minimum.
- Make the organisation register a compact credibility band rather than a visual destination.
- Shorten the TRVLR artefact so it supports the advisory note instead of dominating it.
- Preserve all content, organisation marks, lead actions, reading order and reduced-motion behaviour.
