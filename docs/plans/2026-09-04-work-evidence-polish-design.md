# Work evidence polish — design direction

## Objective

Complete the Work page’s editorial refinement without changing its approved authority narrative. The first desktop viewport should contain the full opening and organisation register; the evidence, founder record and close should feel composed rather than elongated.

## Visual thesis

**A compact private casebook: precise evidence, warm paper, one human portrait at the close.**

## Content plan

1. **Opening and organisation register** — function as one above-fold credibility sequence at a 1400×800 desktop viewport.
2. **Four chapters** — retain all four decisions, shorten supporting copy, align each metric cleanly and replace “Under four months” with the defensible “Up to 50% reduction in delivery time.”
3. **Founder and operator** — retain the founder statement and four ventures, arranged as a balanced 2×2 editorial ledger.
4. **TRVLR note** — retain the current qualified artefact and copy.
5. **Present practice** — add the existing sunglasses portrait in a restrained circular crop beside the private invitation.

## Interaction thesis

- Keep the existing short section and case-row reveal.
- Add a subtle rule expansion to the evidence rows as they enter.
- Give metrics a restrained opacity/position transition; no counters, parallax or decorative motion.

## Evidence integrity

- “Up to 50%” is used rather than “50%+”. It is compatible with the currently stated six-to-eight-month estimate and approximately three-and-a-half-to-four-month early-value range.
- Prior organisations remain clearly qualified as earlier operating work, not clients of the present independent practice.
- No sourced or generated stock imagery is added. The page uses genuine organisation marks, the TRVLR artefact and one existing portrait.

## Responsive behaviour

- At 1400×800, the opening and complete organisation register should fit within the first viewport, including the persistent header.
- Mobile keeps the approved linear reading order; it is not forced into an artificial above-fold target.
- The founder ledger becomes a single column below 720px.
- The circular closing portrait reduces from approximately 170px to 132px on mobile.

## Success criteria

- No horizontal overflow at 1400px, 768px, 414px or 320px.
- Ten organisation marks remain visible.
- Four operating chapters and all four venture entries remain present.
- Body copy stays at or above 13px.
- Mobile menu, reduced-motion fallback and private-conversation dialog remain functional.
