# Cross-page authenticity and impact design

## Objective

Refine the local four-page review so the Work page is easier to navigate, the shared footer feels identical across pages, and the Letter explains how progress can be measured without promising unsupported results.

## Visual thesis

A restrained editorial system: larger but quiet evidence, compact vertical rhythm, and direct links that behave like part of the typography rather than interface chrome.

## Content plan

- Work: link the VaxGuard and Bhuzen venture names, enlarge the prior-organisation marks without increasing the section height, and retain the factual qualifier.
- Footer: use the Work footer as the visual reference and apply one spacing rule to Home, Work, About and Letter.
- Letter: add one cardless measurement passage covering commercial outcome, founder capacity and operating independence.
- All pages: remove generic or repetitive prose only where meaning and evidence are preserved.

## Evidence boundary

- Existing prior-work figures may remain only in the Work and Home evidence sections with their current qualifications.
- The Letter must not imply that coaching guarantees a revenue increase, a percentage improvement or a fixed number of hours returned.
- Revenue or margin, hours per week and founder-dependent decisions are measurement categories. Their baselines and targets are agreed with the client.
- Named organisations, credentials, mentors and quotations retain their current supplied or verified provenance.

## Interaction thesis

Retain the existing restrained reveal and link-hover language. Add no new motion; the refinement should come from scale, alignment and spacing.

## Responsive behaviour

Organisation marks may grow on desktop but remain contained within the two-column mobile register. The measurement passage becomes a single-column ruled list on mobile. Footer alignment and height use the same shared CSS on every page.

## Verification

- Contract tests for venture links, measurement wording, shared stylesheet version and the evidence disclaimer.
- Desktop and compact-viewport checks for overflow, footer placement and section height.
- An editorial integrity pass across all visible page copy.
