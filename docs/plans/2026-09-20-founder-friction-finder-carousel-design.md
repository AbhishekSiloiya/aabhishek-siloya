# Founder Friction Finder Carousel — Design Direction

## Objective

Introduce the live Founder Friction Finder without turning the post into a product advert. The carousel should make a founder pause, recognise the problem, and choose to spend five quiet minutes with the tool.

## Audience

Founders, owners, family-enterprise leaders and senior operators carrying a live growth decision.

## Editorial idea

**Growth rarely stalls where it first appears.**

The carousel begins with the human experience of business friction, separates symptoms from the underlying constraint, and reveals the tool only after the idea has earned attention.

## Five-slide narrative

1. **Growth rarely stalls where it first appears.**
   - Kicker: `A PRIVATE GROWTH REVIEW`
   - Purpose: interrupt the feed with one composed thought.

2. **Pipeline. Margin. Delivery. Founder time.**
   - Supporting line: `Visible symptoms. Not always the real constraint.`
   - Purpose: create recognition without diagnosing the reader.

3. **Six engines. One friction to examine.**
   - Supporting line: `18 evidence-led questions.`
   - Visual: restrained six-engine orbital diagram.
   - Purpose: reveal the method without listing features.

4. **One commercial hypothesis. One focused 30-day move.**
   - Fine print: `No public score. No false benchmark.`
   - Purpose: describe the value and its honest boundary.

5. **Five quiet minutes with the business you are actually building.**
   - Lock-up: `Founder Friction Finder · by Aabhishek Siloya · Powered by Bhuzen`
   - Link: `aabhisheksiloya.com/diagnostic/`
   - Purpose: invitation, not sales close.

## Visual system: Quiet Diagnostic

- Format: 1080 × 1350 px, five pages; usable as Instagram carousel and LinkedIn PDF.
- Palette: warm limestone `#F2EEE5`, near-black `#171512`, oxblood `#6C243D`, muted stone `#8B857B`.
- Typography: high-contrast editorial serif for the principal thought; restrained grotesk/sans for metadata and functional copy. Two families only.
- Composition: asymmetric 12-column grid, generous outer margin, small folio numbers, hairline rules, deliberate line breaks.
- Image use: Aabhishek’s approved black-and-white side profile only on the final page, cropped as a tall editorial fragment rather than a conventional headshot.
- Signature device: a fine oxblood “diagnostic line” that changes position from page to page and resolves into the six-engine orbit on page three.
- Avoid: gold gradients, generic marble, luxury stock images, shadows, rounded SaaS cards, dense paragraphs, mock phone frames and template-like swipe arrows.

## Caption direction

Write as Aabhishek reflecting on why the tool was built. Keep the caption short, candid and useful. Do not repeat all five slides. Avoid “excited to announce”, performance claims and forced engagement questions.

## Evidence boundaries

- The tool contains 18 questions across six business engines.
- It produces a directional growth-friction result, commercial hypothesis and focused 30-day move.
- It is a decision aid, not a diagnosis, forecast or validated peer benchmark.

## Success criteria

- The opening slide is legible in the feed without zooming.
- Each page contains one central idea.
- The sequence feels authored and calm, not AI-generated or template-led.
- The visual system connects to the live site while remaining distinctive enough for an ongoing editorial series.
- Final exports include five 1080 × 1350 PNG files and one five-page PDF.
