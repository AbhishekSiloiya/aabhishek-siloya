# Founder Friction Finder Carousel — Private Counsel Dossier

## Objective

Create a five-slide launch carousel that makes the Founder Friction Finder feel like a confidential piece of private counsel: commercially useful, visually distinctive and recognisably Aabhishek.

The carousel must show the real product, make the outcome obvious and give a founder a clear reason to open the tool.

## Audience

Founders, owners, family-enterprise leaders and senior operators carrying a live question about growth, margin, delivery capacity, leadership bandwidth or continuity.

## Central idea

**Most founders do not need another score. They need to know what is costing them movement.**

The work is presented as a private decision dossier rather than a social-media template. Tool screens become evidence inside the dossier—not decorative browser mock-ups.

## Five-slide story

### 1. The commercial tension

Headline: `What is costing the business movement?`

Support: `The visible problem may be a symptom. The Founder Friction Finder shows you where to look first.`

Visual proof: a tightly cropped executive-mirror result, annotated as an illustrative private brief.

### 2. The evidence

Headline: `Eighteen questions. Six engines. Evidence before instinct.`

Support: `Offer. Demand. Sales. Profit. Delivery. Founder leverage.`

Visual proof: the real assessment interface with one restrained annotation highlighting “closest evidence—not the most flattering answer.”

### 3. The value

Headline: `See what may be exposed—and what may be recoverable.`

Support: `An indicative commercial range based on the supplied revenue band and evidence gap.`

Boundary: `A hypothesis to verify. Not a forecast.`

Visual proof: the actual cost-and-opportunity result, clearly labelled as an illustrative sample.

### 4. The move

Headline: `One move for the next 30 days.`

Support: `A baseline. Weekly actions. Two measures. A decision to keep, adapt or stop.`

Visual proof: the actual tailored plan.

### 5. The invitation

Headline: `Five to six quiet minutes with the business you are actually running.`

Support: `No email to start. No public score. No generic prescription.`

Action: `Run the private review` with `aabhisheksiloya.com/diagnostic/`.

Use Aabhishek’s approved black-and-white side profile as an author mark, not a hero image.

## Visual system

### Private Counsel Dossier

- Material: warm uncoated ivory paper, near-black ink, deep oxblood and restrained antique brass.
- Typography: Cormorant Garamond for selective editorial emphasis; DM Sans for precise working copy. Avoid oversized display type dominating every page.
- Structure: an asymmetric dossier grid with a fixed oxblood evidence rail and changing folio tabs.
- Signature device: a thin “friction line” passes through all five slides. It changes direction at each decision point and resolves into the final CTA.
- Dossier language: small reference marks such as `PRIVATE`, `EVIDENCE`, `VALUE`, `30 DAYS`, `AS / 01–05` and a restrained AS seal.
- Product treatment: real screens are clipped like evidence exhibits, with edge notes, underlines and one annotation per page. No laptop frames, browser chrome recreation or floating SaaS cards.
- Image treatment: the founder portrait appears only on the invitation slide, cropped like an author photograph in a private report.
- Texture: minimal paper grain and blind-rule details. No marble, gradients, generic luxury photography, faux signatures or decorative gold effects.

## Copy standard

- Bold and outcome-led, not cerebral.
- One commercial idea per slide.
- Use short declarative sentences and concrete deliverables.
- Do not imply diagnosis, guaranteed value, peer benchmarking or forecast accuracy.
- The result is a hypothesis to test; the indicative range is not an audited loss.
- Write `Aabhishek Siloya` and `Bhuzen` exactly.

## Launch-post direction

Opening:

`Most founders do not need another score. They need to know what is costing them movement.`

Outcome block:

- identify the first constraint worth verifying;
- see the indicative value that may be exposed; and
- leave with one focused 30-day move.

Close:

`No email to start. No public score. No generic prescription.`

## Evidence and provenance

- Product screens must be generated locally from the production diagnostic source using an illustrative business profile.
- Every illustrative screen must be labelled.
- Aabhishek’s portrait must use the approved existing brand asset.
- No externally sourced decorative imagery is required.
- No test lead data may be transmitted while creating screenshots.

## Success criteria

- The tool is clearly visible on slides one through four.
- A founder can understand the delivered outcome by scanning headlines only.
- The carousel has a recognisable dossier character rather than a generic luxury or AI-template aesthetic.
- All text is legible at phone-feed size.
- Five 1080 × 1350 PNGs and a five-page LinkedIn PDF are produced.
- Publication requires explicit approval of the final artwork and platform copy.
