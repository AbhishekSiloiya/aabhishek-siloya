# Editorial restoration design

## Approved direction

Restore the structure and atmosphere of the original live Editorial landing page. Treat the original as the design source, then polish its spacing, responsiveness and relationship to the main private-counsel website.

## Visual thesis

A dark, photographic editorial salon framed by the main website's restrained navigation and footer. The featured story provides the visual authority; typography and rules organise the collection without making it feel like a generic content platform.

## Homepage Editorial shelf

- Retain the compact three-feature composition.
- Make the entire tile the click target and make that behaviour visually obvious.
- Align article labels, titles and action cues to one baseline system.
- Keep one image-led lead tile and two quieter supporting tiles.
- Use explicit `Read article` or `Open series` cues with a directional arrow.
- Preserve mobile horizontal scrolling with accessible controls.

## Editorial landing page

- Restore the original featured hero with the London image, dark overlay and two-column feature panel.
- Restore the original sequence: featured hero, salon statement, curated feature, selected features, salon note and host.
- Preserve the integrated main-site header instead of the old microsite navigation.
- Keep all article links local to the integrated website.
- Polish spacing and type scale without materially changing the original composition.

## Footer

- Use the compact main-site footer geometry.
- Keep navigation and privacy links legible and balanced.
- Keep `Back to top` at the extreme right on desktop and in the natural reading order on mobile.

## Interaction

- Use subtle image zoom and action-line movement on hover/focus.
- Keep the entire feature/card surface keyboard accessible.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Do not add autoplay or ornamental motion.

## Acceptance criteria

- The landing page opens with the original photographic featured composition rather than a blank introductory panel.
- The original content order and featured-series hierarchy are preserved.
- The integrated main website navigation and compact footer remain present.
- Homepage feature tiles have a consistent, visible action cue and balanced alignment.
- Desktop and mobile layouts retain usable tap targets and no horizontal page overflow.
- Existing publication, lead-form, diagnostic and Editorial integration tests pass.
