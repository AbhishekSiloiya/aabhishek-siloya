What this is

A private advisory relationship for owners and family enterprises that need clearer decisions, roles and conversations around the future of the business.

Where the work begins

Separate the issues without losing the whole.

Commercial questions, leadership questions and family questions often arrive together. The first task is to see which decision belongs where—and who should carry it.

Useful questions

What decisions can this work support?

Questions around business direction, leadership transition, decision rights, operating dependence, growth priorities and continuity.

Is this succession, legal or tax advice?

No. It is strategic and leadership counsel for the business context. Specialist legal, tax, investment and formal succession advice should come from appropriately qualified professionals.

Who should join the first conversation?

Usually the person carrying the decision. Once the issue is clearer, we can decide whether a wider owner, family or leadership conversation would be useful.

Private correspondence
