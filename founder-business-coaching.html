The working approach

Clarity. Growth. Profit.

The CGP System starts by defining the real decision and its constraints. We then examine where growth is credible, what it requires from people and capital, and whether it strengthens the business financially.

This is decision support and coaching. It is not therapy, operational outsourcing, legal advice, tax advice or regulated financial advice.

Useful questions

Who is founder business coaching for?

Founders and owners who are carrying a consequential growth, leadership or continuity decision and want an experienced, independent perspective.

Is this consulting or mentoring?

It is a private advisory relationship with coaching at its centre. The work combines direct questions, operating perspective and practical decision support; it does not replace your management team.

Can we work across markets?

Yes. Aabhishek is based in London and works internationally, informed by delivery experience across the United Kingdom, Europe, Japan and India.
