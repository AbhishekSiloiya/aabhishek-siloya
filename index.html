My philosophy

Good decisions start with what matters to you.

Growth has to make sense for the business and the people behind it. These are the principles I bring to that conversation.

01

Set a clear standard

Be clear about what good looks like, then follow through in the everyday choices.

02

Understand the people

Listen to the people doing the work. Their habits, concerns and relationships affect what a plan can achieve.

03

Lead with integrity

Make decisions you can explain honestly to the people affected by them.

04

Think beyond borders

A new market needs more than the same plan in another language. Understand its culture and how people do business.

05

Build for continuity

Help the business thrive beyond one person, with the skills and responsibility to support the next generation.

06

Keep improving

Keep what works. Be willing to change what does not.

The CGP System

A clear approach to your next decision.

Clarity precedes growthGrowth must become profitProfit creates choice
C

Clarity

Define the decision, your priorities and what must be protected before choosing a direction.

G

Growth

P

Profit

Make sure growth strengthens the business financially and gives you more choice about its future.
