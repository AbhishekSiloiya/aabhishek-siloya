Privacy

A private exchange should remain private.

This notice explains what happens when you use the private-enquiry form on this website. It is intentionally short and specific to that exchange.

What is collected

Your name and email address are required. Depending on the enquiry, you may also provide an organisation, telephone number, decision context or introduction context.

Why it is used

The information is used only to review and respond to your enquiry. It is not added to a marketing list or sold to another organisation.

How it is handled

Form delivery is provided by Web3Forms. Submission records are retained there for 90 days. Email correspondence may remain in the recipient mailbox for as long as the conversation reasonably requires.

What not to send

Please do not include confidential financial, legal or medical information, or private contact details belonging to someone you are introducing.

Your request

You may ask about, correct or request deletion of information sent through the form by emailing aabhisheksiloiya708@gmail.com.

Scope

This website does not currently use advertising trackers, behavioural profiling or a marketing newsletter sign-up.
