Privacy

A private exchange should remain private.

This notice explains how your information is used when you send a private enquiry through this website.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Aabhishek Siloya. Questions about your information can be sent to aabhisheksiloiya708@gmail.com.

What is collected

Your name, email address and message are required. An organisation or family-enterprise name is optional. Please provide only the context needed for a reply.

Purpose and lawful basis

Your information is used to review and respond to your enquiry. The lawful basis is legitimate interests in answering relevant professional enquiries, and—where applicable—steps at your request before entering into a working relationship. It is not added to a marketing list or sold.

Form delivery

Web3Forms processes the form and forwards it to email. Its published documentation says submissions are not stored, although server logs may contain personal information for up to two months. Its servers are in the United States, US-East region, and its parent business is based in India.

Retention

Enquiry emails are reviewed and deleted when no longer needed, normally within 12 months of the last meaningful contact. They may be kept longer where an active working relationship, legal duty or dispute requires it.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your information, restrict its use, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Email the address above. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

What not to send

Please do not include confidential financial, legal, medical or other sensitive information. Obtain permission before sharing another person’s contact details.

Tracking and marketing

This website does not use advertising trackers, behavioural profiling, analytics cookies or a marketing newsletter. Enquiry details are used only for the exchange you initiated.
