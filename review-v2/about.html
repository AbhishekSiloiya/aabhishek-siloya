Global business coach · Founder · Operator

I help founders see the whole decision.

Not just the commercial answer. The people, history, ambition and consequence wrapped around it.

The operating path

I learnt this work from inside the business.

For more than two decades, I have worked across product, digital strategy and transformation—in businesses where customer trust, brand experience and commercial performance had to move together.

Selected prior work has taken me from Heathrow and Japan Airlines to Maybourne Hotel Group, Sonepar and ASDA. Different cultures. Different operating models. The same lesson: ambition is rarely the scarce resource. Clarity usually is.

Building Bhuzen and VaxGuard changed the view again. As a founder, the decision is no longer an elegant recommendation. It is yours to fund, explain and live with.

The advice became better when the consequences became mine.

Why coaching

The answer is not the work.

I am not trying to be the cleverest person in the room. My work is to hold the whole context, ask the question others may avoid and notice when the obvious answer is protecting an old assumption.

Good counsel does not borrow the founder's authority. It sharpens judgment, tests the trade-offs and leaves the final decision exactly where it belongs.

The private room

Direct questions. No theatre.

Privacy is not a line in a service description. It is what allows the real question to enter the room.
