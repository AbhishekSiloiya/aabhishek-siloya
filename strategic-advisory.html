Useful questions

When is strategic advisory useful?

When a founder or owner faces a consequential choice about growth, market expansion, investment priorities, decision rights or the operating model behind the strategy.

Do you deliver transformation programmes?

The current practice centres on independent counsel and decision support. The selected work reflects earlier delivery experience; it is evidence of operating perspective, not a promise of outsourced implementation.

How does an engagement begin?

With a short private note about the decision, its context and timing. A first conversation establishes whether the work and the relationship are a fit.

Private correspondence

Start with the decision, not the deck.

Share enough context to begin. The first exchange is used to understand what is at stake and whether an independent perspective would help.
